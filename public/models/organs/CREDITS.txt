organs.glb — anatomical organs and blood vessels shown inside the Self figure

Source: HRA 3D Reference Object Library, Human Reference Atlas (HuBMAP / NIH),
https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library
Created by Kristen Browne, Heidi Schlehlein et al., Cyberinfrastructure for Network
Science Center, Indiana University; built from the Visible Human Male (U.S. National
Library of Medicine). Brain: Allen Institute based reference brain (brain-male v1.4).

Models used (male): brain v1.4, heart v1.3, lung v1.4, spinal cord v1.1,
small intestine v1.2, large intestine v1.3, blood vasculature v1.3.
