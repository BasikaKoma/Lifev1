About

A workspace for your next move

lifev1 helps you organize your life with intention. Whether you're building a business, improving your health, or designing a better daily routine, everything lives in one calm, focused environment.

Your data stays yours. lifev1 only accesses third-party services — like Oura — when you explicitly connect them, and you can disconnect at any time.
